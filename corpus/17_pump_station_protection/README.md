# 17_pump_station_protection

Pump station, single pump protection. Low suction pressure held for a delay and high discharge
pressure both latch a trip in an RS. A priming pulse (TP) opens the prime valve at each start, a
cool-down (TOF) keeps the cooling valve open after stopping and blocks restarts, and a CTU limits
the number of starts until the counter is reset.

Stimulus: generated by `corpus/generate_stimuli.py`.
