# 16_pump_station_level

Pump station, wet-well level control. Two pumps, lead and lag. Lead starts at START_LEVEL, lag joins
at LAG_LEVEL after a delay, both stop at STOP_LEVEL. Below DRY_LEVEL pumps are forced off to protect
against dry running. High-high level needs to persist before it alarms. The lead rotates each cycle.

Stimulus: generated by `corpus/generate_stimuli.py`.
